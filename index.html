Stone-ground chocolate, wrapped in banana leaf, rooted in home soil.

Sunburst's Balay Tablea keeps Cabatuan's cacao tradition alive the old way: beans from local farming families, ground by hand on a stone metate, rolled and wrapped in banana leaf, brewed into tsokolate the way it's always been.

Our Story

A teacher's bet on her town's cacao.

In 2012, retired public school teacher Catherine Taleon put her own performance bonus into reviving Cabatuan's forgotten cacao craft — choosing a traditional stone grinder over modern machines, and training local farmers and out-of-school youth along the way. Today, Sunburst's Balay Tablea is the authorized manufacturer and distributor of Tan Tono Tablea, Cabatuan's official heritage chocolate.
