Ordering

Placing an order is a request to buy. Prices and stock are confirmed by our system when you place the order; the total shown on your confirmation is the amount due.

Payment

Payment options depend on how you get your order: cash on delivery, pay on pickup, GCash or bank transfer. For GCash and bank transfer, please use your order number as the payment reference. [Owner to confirm accepted methods.]

Pickup and delivery

Pickup is at our Cabatuan shop; we will tell you when your order is ready. Delivery fees are shown at checkout. Delivery times depend on your location. [Owner to confirm delivery areas and timing.]

Cancellations and refunds

Contact us as soon as possible to cancel. Unpaid online-payment orders may be cancelled automatically if payment is not received in time. When an order is cancelled, its items return to stock. [Owner to confirm refund rules.]

Allergens

Our products are made from cacao and may be produced in a kitchen that also handles milk, nuts and gluten. Please ask before ordering if you have allergies.
