Recognition

Cabatuan's cacao, on wider stages.

From a government workshop visit to an international expo floor, here's the trail — with sources.

In the Press & On Record

A decade of milestones.

  • 2018

    DOST Secretary visits the Cabatuan workshop

    DOST Secretary Fortunato dela Peña toured the operation and encouraged Catherine Taleon to keep refining the product's texture — feedback that shaped the years of upgrades that followed.

    Read the DOST Region VI post →
  • 2019

    Featured by Daily Guardian & Iloilo Today

    Both outlets profiled the business as a model for how technology and tradition can grow a local food industry, highlighting Catherine's dual life as teacher and chocolate-maker.

    Read Daily Guardian's feature →
  • 2018–19

    Top-rated seller on oneSTore PH

    With DOST support, Balay Tablea's tablea with muscovado sugar became a top-rated product on the government's oneSTore PH e-commerce platform for MSMEs.

    Read Iloilo Today's report →
  • 2022

    Joins LaMeza Ilonggo Degustation

    Balay Tablea's cacao becomes part of a curated, multi-course Iloilo tasting menu experience celebrating Ilonggo ingredients and flavor.

  • 2024

    UNESCO Cities of Gastronomy Expo, Macao

    Balay Tablea represents Iloilo on an international stage; its banana-leaf-wrapped tablea rolls sell out before the expo ends.

  • 2025

    Second Macao trip & a national feature

    Catherine returns to Macao for cooking demos with Richmonde Hotel Iloilo's Chef Ariel Castañeda Jr. and Megaworld's Chef Panky Lopez, and Simpol.ph publishes a national feature on the brand.

    Read the Simpol.ph feature →
Culinary Collaborations

On the menu at Richmonde Hotel Iloilo.

Tablea Cheesecake with Tultul

A collaboration with Executive Chef Ariel Castañeda Jr., pairing dark Ilonggo cacao with native sea salt (tultul). It's since become a signature dessert on the hotel's menu.

Ilonggo Tiramisu

Local coffee layered with Balay Tablea cacao, reimagining a classic Italian dessert with regional soul — served alongside the cheesecake as a hotel signature.
