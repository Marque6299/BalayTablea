Visit Us

Minutes from Iloilo Airport.

Watch the roasting and grinding, try the stone metate yourself, and sit down to a proper Ilonggo merienda.

At The Workshop

Book ahead, then pull up a stool.

Cabatuan sits about 24 kilometers northwest of Iloilo City and is home to Iloilo International Airport, so a stop at Balay Tablea works well right before a flight or straight after landing. Book ahead and we'll walk you through roasting and grinding through our viewing window, then serve mangga't ibos with hot tsokolate — the same welcome merienda featured in Simpol.ph's coverage of the shop.

Beyond the Shop

See where the cacao actually grows.

Cabatuan's local government organizes experiential farm visits for tourists who want to see cacao grown, harvested, and turned into tablea firsthand — a natural pairing with a workshop stop. Ask us when you book and we'll help coordinate both in one trip.

Before You Come

Common questions.

Do I need to book ahead?

Yes — message us on Facebook or use the contact form at least a day ahead so we can plan the roasting/grinding demo around your visit.

Can you host group tours or school trips?

Yes. Larger groups just need more lead time so we can prepare enough tsokolate and organize the metate demonstration in batches.

Is it kid-friendly?

Yes — the grinding demo and merienda are popular with families, and it pairs well with the LGU's farm visit for a full-day itinerary.

Can I combine this with a farm visit?

Yes, ask when you book — the Cabatuan LGU coordinates farm visits and we're happy to help line up both stops on the same day.

Do you sell on-site?

Yes — tablea rolls, hot tsokolate mix, cacao nibs, pasalubong gift sets, and otap are all available at the shop.
