Privacy Notice

What we collect when you order, book a visit or write to us, and what we do with it.

Draft for owner review. This page was written as a plain-language starting point. Sunburst's Balay Tablea should review it (ideally with a lawyer) and replace anything that does not match how the business actually works before relying on it.

Last updated: 28 September 2026.

Sunburst's Balay Tablea (“we”) handles your personal information under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

What we collect

  • Orders: name, email, mobile number, delivery address (delivery only), the items you order and your chosen payment method.
  • Visit bookings: name, email, mobile number, visit date, group size and notes you add.
  • Inquiries: name, email, mobile number, business or farm name and your message.

You never need an account. We do not ask for card numbers on this website.

Why we collect it

To prepare and deliver your order, confirm your booking, reply to your message, send order or booking updates, and keep business records.

Who sees it

Our team, and the service providers that host this website and send email on our behalf (our database host and email provider). We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

Order and booking records are kept for as long as we need them for accounting and customer service. [Owner to confirm retention period.]

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details below.
